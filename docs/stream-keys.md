# Stream keys

A **stream key** is like a password that tells YouTube or Facebook "this video belongs to Glad Tidings Church." [[Streamlabs]] uses it to send our stream to the right place.

> ⚠️ **Never share a stream key** in a text, email, or screenshot. Anyone with it can stream to our channel.

## Verifying keys (Thursday)

1. Open [[Streamlabs]]. [[open:streamlabs]]
2. Make sure the church YouTube channel and Facebook page are both connected.
3. Click **Go Live** and check that both platforms show up and are turned on. Close the window without going live.

## When a key might change

- Someone reset it on YouTube or Facebook.
- The YouTube or Facebook account was disconnected or logged out in Streamlabs.

If the stream won't start, see [[troubleshooting|Troubleshooting]].
