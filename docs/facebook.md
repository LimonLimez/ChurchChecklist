# Facebook

We stream every Sunday service to the church Facebook page at the same time as [[YouTube]], using [[Streamlabs]].

> ✏️ **To fill in:** the link to our Facebook page, and who is an admin.

## Title and description

Use the **same title and description** as the [[YouTube]] page.

## Checking that we're live

Open the church page in the Facebook app on a phone. The live video should be at the top of the page. Check that you can **see and hear** it.

## After the stream

Facebook saves the video to the page after the stream ends. Check it shows up, and fix the title if it's wrong.
