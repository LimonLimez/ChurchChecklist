# Going live

**We go live after worship ends**, not at the start of service.

[[timeline:sunday]]

## Step by step

1. **Before service:** open [[Streamlabs]] and check that the **Main OUTPUT** scene is selected and the camera shows in the preview. [[open:streamlabs]]
2. **Worship ends:** click **Go Live**. Type the title and description (see below). Check that **YouTube** and **Facebook** are both switched on and **Record Stream** is on, then click **Confirm & Go Live**.
3. **Check it worked:** look on a phone. You should see and hear the stream on both [[YouTube]] and [[Facebook]].
4. **Sermon:** run the slides and scripture in [[ProPresenter]]. To show live viewers what is on the main screens, switch Streamlabs to **Audience View**. Switch back to **Main OUTPUT** for the camera. Glance at the status bar for dropped frames.
5. **Service is over:** click **End Stream**. While we're live, the Go Live button changes to End Stream.
6. **Check the video saved** on YouTube and Facebook. The recording on the MacBook is under **Recordings** in the Streamlabs side menu.

**Title:** the date, a dash, then GT CHURCH, like `October 18, 2026 - GT CHURCH`. **Description:** the CCLI line from the [[YouTube]] page.

> **Video needed:** a screen recording of going live and ending a stream.

Use the [[checklist:sunday]] so nothing gets missed.
