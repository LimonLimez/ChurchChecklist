# The media MacBook

All media team software runs on the church MacBook.

## Before service

- Plug in the charger.
- Turn on **Do Not Disturb** (Control Center at the top right of the screen → Focus) so notifications don't pop up on the screens.
- Connect to the church network. Use a cable if one is available.

## Opening this wiki

Double-click **Start Wiki** on the desktop. A window opens (leave it open), then the wiki opens in the browser.

## After service

Clear the screens in [[ProPresenter]], close [[Streamlabs]], and turn off the projectors/TVs.
