**ProPresenter** is the program that puts words and pictures on the screens in the sanctuary. It also sends slides to the live stream through [[Streamlabs]].

[[open:propresenter]]

## The main parts of the screen

> **Screenshot needed:** the main ProPresenter window with arrows pointing at the Library, the Playlist, the slide area, and the Clear buttons. Save it as `docs/images/propresenter-main.png`.

| Part | What it does |
|------|--------------|
| **Library** | Every song and presentation we've ever made. Search here before making a new song. |
| **Playlist** | The list of items for one service, in order. We make one playlist per Sunday. |
| **Slides** | Click a slide to put it on the screen. |
| **Clear buttons** | Remove things from the screen. Use these if the wrong thing shows up. |

## Words you'll hear

- **Presentation**: one song or one set of slides.
- **Arrangement**: the order of a song's parts (verse, chorus, bridge). See [[lyrics|Lyrics]].
- **Audience screen**: what the congregation sees.
- **Stage screen**: what the band and pastor see (often shows the next slide).
- **Lower third**: words on the bottom of the screen, like a name or scripture. See [[glossary|Glossary]].
