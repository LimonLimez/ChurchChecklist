**ProPresenter** is the program that puts words and pictures on the screens in the sanctuary. We use it for worship lyrics, scripture, sermon slides, and announcements. The live stream in [[Streamlabs]] shows ProPresenter's slides during the sermon.

## Words you'll hear

- **Library**: where every song and presentation is saved. Search here before making a new song.
- **Playlist**: everything for one service, in order. We make one per Sunday, named with the date.
- **Presentation**: one song or one set of slides.
- **Arrangement**: the order of a song's parts (verse, chorus, bridge). See [[lyrics|Lyrics]].
- **Audience screen**: what the congregation sees.
- **Stage screen**: what the band and pastor see, usually the current and next slide.

## Clearing the screen

If the wrong thing shows up, don't panic. The clear buttons next to the preview take things off the screen:

| Button | What it removes |
|--------|-----------------|
| **Clear All** | Everything. Use it when in doubt. |
| **Clear slide** | Just the words (lyrics, scripture, sermon slide) |
| **Clear media** | Backgrounds and videos |
| **Clear props / messages** | Logos, lower thirds, and on-screen messages |
| **Clear audio** | Music or sound playing from ProPresenter |
