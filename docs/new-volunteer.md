# New volunteer guide

Thanks for serving! Here is the order to learn things in. Each step should take one or two Sundays.

1. **Shadow someone.** Sit with an experienced team member on a Sunday and watch them use the [[checklist:sunday]].
2. **Learn [[ProPresenter]].** Practice moving through lyrics during a rehearsal.
3. **Learn Thursday prep.** Do the [[checklist:thursday]] with someone helping you.
4. **Learn [[Streamlabs]].** Learn the scenes and [[going-live|how to go live]].
5. **Run a Sunday yourself**, with someone nearby as backup.

> **Golden rule:** If something goes wrong, stay calm, put up a safe slide (the church logo), and ask for help. Check [[troubleshooting|Troubleshooting]].
